# How to Access Logs

Use this when you need to review the logs to determine an issue or provide them to a member of the Eglantine team.

**[See here](https://evilperson1337.notion.site/How-to-Access-Logs-2b057c2edaf68105a281fe1688a332d4) for an illustrated guide (written for Eden).**

---

## Pre-Requisites

- Eglantine installed and run at least once

---

## Steps

###  Windows

*By default the Eglantine folder is stored in your AppData `C:\Users\<USER>\AppData\Roaming\hortensia\log`, or the local **user** folder if you have a portable installation.*

<aside>

1. Open Eglantine.
2. Navigate to *File > Open Eglantine Folders > Log Folder*, a Windows explorer window will appear.
3. Your log files are now here for your review or upload.
</aside>

### Steam Deck

*By default the Eglantine folder is stored in `/home/deck/.local/share/hortensia`, or the local **user** folder if you have a portable installation.*

<aside>

1. Open Eglantine.
2. Navigate to *File > Open Eglantine Folders > Log Folder*, a Dolphin file explorer window will appear.
3. Your log files are now here for your review or upload.
</aside>

### Android

*Logs are stored in the application data, so you wouldn't be able to access the files directly without a rooted device.*

<aside>

1. Open the Eglantine application on your device.
2. Navigate to *Settings > Share Debug Logs.* You can either save it to your device or send it through some other manner supported by your device.
</aside>
